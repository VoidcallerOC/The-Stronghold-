Four Ways To Play
The Stronghold Way

Whether you're chasing a grail card, cashing in a collection, or just looking for a table and good people — the door's open. This is a place built for players, by players.

01

Buy

Singles, sealed booster boxes, decks, supplies and hard-to-find collectibles — fresh inventory every week.

02

Sell

Turn your cards into cash or store credit. Bring in a single, a binder, or a whole collection for a fair offer.

03

Trade

Level up your deck and your collection. Trade with the shop and the community to find exactly what you need.

04

Play

Open tables, weekly events and tournaments. Draft night, Commander, league play — pull up a chair.
